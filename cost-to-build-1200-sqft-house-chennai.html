Cost to build a 1200 sq ft house in Chennai

Home / FAQ / 1200 Sq Ft Cost Guide

The Short Answer

₹25.79 – ₹34.19 lakhs, all-inclusive

Building a 1200 sq.ft house in Chennai costs between ₹25.79 lakhs and ₹34.19 lakhs in 2026, depending on the finish level you choose. These are all-inclusive package rates — GST, materials and labour included — with no hidden charges. Here is exactly how that breaks down.

What Changes The Price

Five factors that move the final number

  • Marshy or filled-up soil (common in parts of North Chennai) may need deeper footings or piles, adding ₹1–3 lakhs
  • A free soil assessment during the site visit fixes this before quoting
  • 1200 sq.ft as G+1 (600 + 600) costs slightly more per sq.ft than a single floor due to staircase, slab and structural work
  • But it leaves ground space open and supports future expansion
  • The jump from Basic to Premium (₹700/sq.ft) is almost entirely finishes: tiles, sanitaryware, elevation, paint brands
  • Structure quality stays the same in every package — we never compromise there
  • CMDA/DTCP plan approval, EB and water/sewer connections are charged at actuals — typically ₹0.5–1.5 lakhs depending on locality
  • Itemised transparently in your estimate
  • Narrow-road plots increase material handling cost slightly
  • We build across all of Chennai — Kathirvedu, Madhavaram, Ambattur, Avadi, Porur, Tambaram and beyond
Timeline & Process

8–10 months, milestone by milestone

A 1200 sq.ft single-floor home takes 8 to 10 months from approved plan to handover (10–12 months for G+1).
